FAQ Schema

How long do golf cart batteries last?▼

Electric golf cart batteries typically last 5-7 years with proper maintenance. Lithium batteries can last 10+ years.

Do I need a driver's license?▼

Requirements vary by state. Many allow golf carts on roads without a license if speed is under 25mph. Check local regulations.

Can golf carts go uphill?▼

Yes, but range decreases on hills. Electric carts handle slopes up to 25% grade. Consider a lifted cart for hilly terrain.
