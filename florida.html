Florida Golf Cart Laws

Do I need a driver's license?▼

Golf carts can be driven on roads with speed limits under 35 mph without a driver's license in Florida. For faster roads, a Class E license is required.

Can I drive on the road?▼

Yes, on roads posted 35 mph or less. Golf carts must have safety lights for nighttime driving.

Do I need insurance?▼

While not required by law, we strongly recommend golf cart insurance for protection.
