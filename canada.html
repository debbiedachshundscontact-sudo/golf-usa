Canadian Golf Cart Laws by Province

Ontario Requirements▼

Registration required for road use. Golf carts allowed on roads posted 40km/h or less. Require $1M liability insurance.

Alberta Requirements▼

Golf cartsallowed on public lands. Municipality regulations vary. Some require registration as "off-highway vehicles."

British Columbia Requirements▼

ICBC registration available. Must display slow vehicle sign. Not allowed on highways.
